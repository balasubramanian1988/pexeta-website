The service

What we run for you

Most sellers either run ads on autopilot or hand them to someone who tweaks bids once a week. Neither wins. Amazon advertising rewards daily, disciplined management — the kind that only makes sense when the person running it has actually sold on the platform.

Pexeta takes full ownership of your Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display campaigns. We build the structure, set the strategy, and manage the account day to day so every rupee of spend works toward profit, not just clicks.

Campaign structure & strategy

We architect your campaigns around your catalog, margins and goals — not a generic template.

Bid & budget optimisation

Daily bid and budget management to hit your ACoS targets and stop wasted spend.

Dayparting

Ads run when your buyers convert — not burning budget on dead hours.

Negative keyword harvesting

We continuously cut the search terms draining budget without returning sales.

Placement & targeting control

Product, keyword and audience targeting managed to put spend where it performs.

Transparent reporting

Clear reporting on spend, ACoS and what changed — performance, not vanity metrics.
